ALBA HEAT is an original open-world crime game set in Puerto Alba, a coastal city caught in permanent sunset. You play Marina Solano, a cab driver working the dawn shift, who agrees to carry a stranger's suitcase from Tito at the marina across town to the Hotel Alba — a simple fare that turns into a run from the Alba PD. Explore the procedurally built city on foot or behind the wheel, weave through traffic and pedestrians, and lift any parked car you can reach while the heat builds around you.

A five-star wanted system drives pursuits and roadblocks as your crimes stack up, and you can fight back with fists or a pistol. Three story missions carry Marina's run from the marina pickup to the final delivery, framed by a HUD and live minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound, and a localStorage save that remembers your progress and your car. The game is plain static files — three.js is vendored, and there is nothing to install or build.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory game
# then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser
```
